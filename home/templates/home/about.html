About Seekr

A calm platform connecting early-career talent with recruiters — professional without the corporate noise.

What we believe

Job searching should feel human. Seekr borrows the calm, evidence-first visual language of modern institutional software — white surfaces, restrained blue, and clear hierarchy.

Job seekers

Build a profile, search with real filters, apply with a note, and track Applied → Offer.

Recruiters

Post roles, review applicants, save searches, and get recommendations with reasons.

Administrators

Manage users and roles so the platform stays fair and safe.
