About Seekr

Seekr is a job board for students and recent graduates, and for the recruiters hiring them. It was built as a CS 2340 project at Georgia Tech.

If you're looking for work

Make a profile with your education, experience, projects, and links. Search jobs by skills, location, salary, work model, and visa sponsorship, apply with a short note, and follow each application from Applied to Offer. Your privacy settings decide which recruiters can see your profile.

If you're hiring

Post and edit roles, search candidates by skills, location, school, and projects, and review each applicant's profile and note on one page. Save a search to get an alert when someone new matches, and invite recommended candidates to apply.

Administration

Administrators can change user roles and suspend accounts.
