About

Seekr allows dedicated students to find job postings that match their goals while building their own personal profile, helping recruiters better understand specific applicants needs. Candidates can see exactly where they stand in the process instead of being ghosted for months. Other resources like LinkedIn have algorithms and features that can dilute the application process, serving more as a social networking or media sites.

Furthermore, Seekr makes recruiters' lives simpler by providing them with a platform to manage applicants, save candidate searches, and recieve recommendations rather than manually filter through prospective employees. Overall, Seekr turns the application process from a noisy system to a purposed search, helping match compatible applicants and recruiters.
