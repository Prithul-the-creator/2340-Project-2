About

LinkedIn is a social networking site that happens to post jobs, but it's driven by algorithms and features that can dilute the application process. Seekr allows dedicated students to find postings that match their needs and share notes about why they are drawn to a specific role, helping recruiters understand their needs better. Candidates can see exactly where they stand in the process instead of being ghosted for months.

Furthermore, it makes recruiters' lives easier by giving them explainable recommendations rather than a black-box score, and enabling them to save candidate searches. Overall, Seekr turns the application process from a noisy, luck-based gauntlet to a purposed search for a fit that both seekers and recruiters find compatible.
