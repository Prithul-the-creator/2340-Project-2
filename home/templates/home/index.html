Frequently asked questions

How Seekr works for job seekers, recruiters, and administrators.

  • Who is Seekr for?

    Early-career job seekers, recruiters hiring interns and new grads, and admins who keep the platform fair.

    Can I control my privacy?

    Yes. Choose public to recruiters, visible only to jobs you apply to, or fully private.

  • What can recruiters do?

    Post jobs, search candidates, review applications, save searches with alerts, and get recommendations with reasons.

    How does applying work?

    One click shares your profile (and resume if uploaded) plus an optional note up to 500 characters.

  • What statuses exist?

    Applied, Review, Interview, Offer, and Closed. Seekers see every status change their recruiter makes.

    How are candidates recommended?

    Seekr compares a job's required skills with each candidate's skills, projects, and location, and shows the reasons next to every suggestion.
